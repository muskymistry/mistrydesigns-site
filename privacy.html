Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Mistry Designs ("I", "me") makes apps for Apple platforms and runs this website. This policy explains what information is collected and how it is used. The short version: I don't sell your data, and I collect as little as possible.

Apps

Unless an app's App Store listing or this page says otherwise, Mistry Designs apps:

  • Do not require an account
  • Do not collect, store or share personal information on my servers
  • Do not contain third-party advertising or tracking
  • Keep your data on your device, or in your own iCloud account if you turn on iCloud sync

If a future app needs to collect any information, this policy and that app's App Store privacy label will be updated before release to explain exactly what is collected and why.

Crash reports and analytics

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide me with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These don't identify you and are used only to fix bugs and improve the apps.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. I never receive your payment details.

This website

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or third-party trackers. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and pages requested, for security and reliability.

Email

If you email me, I'll use your address and message only to reply to you. I won't add you to a mailing list or share your details.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

You can ask what information I hold about you, or ask me to delete it, by emailing support@mistrydesigns.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@mistrydesigns.com
