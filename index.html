01 · Equipment rack

Travelling video production rack

A lightweight sheet-metal rack for live production gear: two ATEM switchers, a Teranex Mini converter with Smart Panel, and two UltraStudio playback units. It's designed in Fusion as laser-cut, bent steel instead of heavy extrusion. Everything inside runs on universal 100–240 V power, so the rack works wherever it travels.

Slim Thunderbolt PCIe enclosure

This repackages a three-slot Thunderbolt PCIe expansion chassis and two DeckLink Duo 2 capture cards into a portable custom box only 32 mm thick. It's made of powder-coated steel with PEM inserts, and an internal flat power supply replaces the external brick.

Stage tools & apps

Built for live events.

I design hardware and software for live events. Each tool is built around a real problem I've run into backstage. Two are in development now, and apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac are coming soon. Each will be listed here with its own support and privacy details.

Device · In development

Stage Clock

A portable, battery-powered stage clock with a 10.1-inch touchscreen on a fold-out leg. It runs on a Raspberry Pi 5 and standard NP-F camera batteries, and shows presenters and crew the time and what's left in each segment. It has a wireless link and a 3D-printed enclosure.

Raspberry Pi + Blackout · Working, v7

BeatBridge

Music-reactive lighting for live events. It takes a feed from the mixing desk, splits it into up to twelve frequency bands, and sends OSC to the Blackout lighting console app on iPad, which drives the DMX fixtures over Art-Net. The lights shift colour as each band gets louder. Up to six "hit bands" punch to a colour on sudden accents. It's built for human-played music with no fixed tempo, so it follows the performance rather than flashing on a beat.

  • Up to 12 bands
  • Saved shows
  • Touchscreen + phone control
  • No cloud
