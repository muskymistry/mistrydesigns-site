Frequently asked questions

How do I request a refund?
App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund.

How do I restore a purchase?
Where an app offers in-app purchases, open its settings and tap "Restore Purchases" while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Can I suggest a feature?
Yes, please do. Send ideas to the support address above.
